How it's built

This page is a hand‑built index over a verbatim export of the course's Brandeis Moodle site, taken 21 October 2026. Every link above opens the original file from the export — nothing has been re‑hosted or re‑typeset. The raw Moodle front page is still here too, at moodle-export.html.

Four third‑party textbooks referenced in Part II and Part III (Speech and Language Processing, Distributional Semantics, Geometric Algebra for Computer Science, and the van Eijck & Unger course text) are marked external — they're under third‑party copyright and aren't redistributed in this repository. Each links out to where you can get it instead.
